Telehealth Consultation Hub

Welcome to MediCare's secure telehealth portal. Our virtual consultation service brings premium medical advice, discussions, and prescriptions directly to you. To ensure patient safety and proper medical scheduling, all video and audio calls are accessed strictly through a pre-booked appointment schedule. Explore our active specialists below, secure your slot, and connect at your scheduled time.

How to Consult a Doctor Online

1

Select Doctor

Choose an active specialist from the directory list below.

2

Book Appointment

Fill the form, choose Video/Audio Mode, and select your slot.

3

Go to Details

Wait for doctor approval, then view your appointment details.

4

Start Secure Call

Click "Join Call" at the scheduled time to connect securely.
